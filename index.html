Privacy Policy

Word Puzzle — Last updated: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Word Puzzle ("the app", "we", "us") handles information. The app is published by BitNova. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. We do not run our own servers that store your personal data.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves your game progress (such as level, score, coins, achievements, purchased themes, chosen language, and sound setting) only on your device. This data is not sent to us. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, this information is deleted.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads using Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use certain information from your device, such as:

  • Advertising ID (a resettable identifier for your device)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device information (model, operating system version, language)
  • Ad interactions and app diagnostics (for example, ad views, clicks and crash information)

This information is collected by Google, not by us. How Google uses it is described in Google's Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

4. Consent in the European Economic Area and the UK

If you are in the EEA or the UK, the app asks for your consent before showing ads, using Google's consent tool (User Messaging Platform). You can change your choice at any time from the in-app menu (Privacy settings), when it is available for your region.

5. Rewarded ads

The app offers optional rewarded ads (for example, to earn free coins). Watching them is voluntary.

6. Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, please contact us and we will help.

7. Data sharing and security

We do not sell your personal information. Data handled by Google AdMob is transmitted over secure connections and is subject to Google's policies. Because we do not store your personal data on our servers, there is no personal data held by us to delete or export.

8. Your choices

  • Reset your Advertising ID or limit ad personalization in your device settings.
  • Change your consent choice in the app menu (EEA/UK).
  • Delete all game data on your device by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, and the "Last updated" date above will change.
